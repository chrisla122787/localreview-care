The service

The service costs $149 per month, billed monthly in advance, month-to-month.

You grant access by adding us as a manager on your profile; you keep ownership of your profile at all times and may remove our access at any time. Removing access or revoking authorization ends our ability to provide the service.

Approvals

Review responses are drafts until you approve them. Nothing is posted to your profile in your name without your approval, except under a written approval arrangement you have agreed to in advance (for example, pre-approved response templates for 4- and 5-star reviews). Responses to reviews of 3 stars or fewer, and all sensitive complaints, are always held for your explicit approval.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate business information and tell us when it changes (hours, phone, services, closures).
  • Respond to approval requests and escalations in a reasonable time — unanswered reviews wait on your approval.
  • Keep your Google account credentials to yourself; never share your password with us. We will never ask for it.
