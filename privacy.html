Privacy Policy

LocalReview Care — last updated November 2026

LocalReview Care provides Google Business Profile and review management services to local businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what we do with it. Plainly: we collect only what we need to run the service, and we never sell your information.

What we collect

  • Client business information: business name, contact name, email address, phone number, business address, and Google Business Profile details — provided by you in the onboarding form.
  • Profile and review data: your public Google reviews, ratings, and profile information, which we monitor and report on as part of the service. This is public information displayed on Google.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number; Stripe handles payment data under its own privacy policy.
  • Website contact: if you email us, we keep that correspondence so we can answer you.

How we use it

  • To monitor your Google reviews and profile, draft responses for your approval, publish approved content, and send your monthly report.
  • To communicate with you about your service, approvals, and escalations.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.
  • We never post anything to your Google profile without your authorization and approval, as described in our Terms.
  • We never use reviewer names or review content for anything other than providing your service and reports.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers needed to operate (such as Stripe for payments and our email provider), and only to the extent needed. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and your choices

We keep client records while you are a client and for a reasonable period afterward for accounting and legal purposes. You may ask us at any time what information we hold about your business and ask us to correct or delete it (subject to legal record-keeping requirements) by emailing us.
