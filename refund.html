Refund Policy

LocalReview Care — last updated November 2026

Simple and honest, like the service:

Monthly service, cancel anytime

LocalReview Care is $149/month, billed monthly in advance, month-to-month. You can cancel at any time by emailing us — no cancellation fee, no notice period beyond the current month.

What happens when you cancel

  • We keep providing the service until the end of the month you have already paid for.
  • You are never charged again after that month.

Refunds

  • A month already in service is not refundable. Once a billing month has begun and the service is being provided, that month's $149 is earned and is not refunded if you cancel partway through.
  • If we never started, you get it back. If you paid but we have not begun providing the service for that month (for example, onboarding/authorization was never completed), we will refund that month in full.
  • Billing errors are always fixed. If you were charged twice, charged after your cancellation took effect, or charged the wrong amount, we will refund the incorrect charge in full as soon as we confirm it.

How to cancel or ask about a charge

Email hello@getlocalreviewcare.com from the email address on your account. Cancellations are confirmed by email so you have it in writing.
